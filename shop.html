How this becomes real

Connect a store. Keep the look.

Can people buy already?

Not yet. No payment provider or print-on-demand account is connected, so the site intentionally labels products as coming soon.

What can we connect?

Fourthwall, Printful, Printify, Shopify, Etsy or another storefront can handle products and checkout while this GitHub Pages site remains the branded front door.

Will the design need rebuilding?

No. Product photos, prices and Buy buttons can replace the concept content inside these same cards.
