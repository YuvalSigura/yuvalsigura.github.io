Creamy chili pasta

The goal: big comfort-food energy with very little cleanup.

What you need

  • 250 g pasta
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 1–2 tsp chili flakes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 150 ml cooking cream
  • Parmesan, salt, pepper and a little pasta water

How I make it

Boil the pasta until just shy of done. In a wide pan, gently cook the garlic and chili in olive oil, then fry the tomato paste until it darkens slightly. Add cream and a splash of pasta water, stir until glossy, then finish the pasta in the sauce. Parmesan goes in off the heat. Taste before adding more salt.
