Cooking

Creamy tomato pasta for the night you cannot be bothered

The idea: garlic, tomato paste, cream, parmesan and pasta water. It tastes like more effort than it is.

  • Boil pasta and save a mug of the water.
  • Cook garlic in olive oil, add tomato paste and let it darken slightly.
  • Add cream, parmesan and enough pasta water to make it glossy.
  • Toss the pasta through, add pepper, chili flakes and basil if you have it.

Makeup

Soft glam that still looks like you

The formula: light base, cream blush, brushed brows, brown liner, mascara and a glossy lip. The goal is polished, not covered.

  • Spot-conceal instead of masking the whole face.
  • Keep blush high and blend it back toward the temples.
  • Use brown liner close to the lashes for definition without a hard edge.
  • Finish with gloss and stop before “one more thing” becomes seven more things.
